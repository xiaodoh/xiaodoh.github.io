👋 About me

I am Qiang Xiao, Associate Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Automation, Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My current research focuses on the theory of cooperative control for autonomous unmanned systems. My research interests include time-scale calculus theory, integrated longitudinal and lateral control of vehicle platoons, and reinforcement learning theory and applications.

I have published multiple papers in journals such as IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, Automatica, and SIAM Journal on Comtrol and Optimization.
